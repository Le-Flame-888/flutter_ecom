Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our policy

How long do I have to return?

expand_more

You have 30 days from the date of delivery to return your items for a full refund. Items must be in original condition with all tags still attached and in their original packaging.

Is return shipping free?

expand_more

Yes! We offer free return shipping on all domestic orders. Simply use our return portal to generate and print your prepaid shipping label.

When will I get my refund?

expand_more

Once your return is received and inspected (usually within 48 hours of receipt), your refund will be processed and a credit will be automatically applied to your original method of payment within 5-10 business days.

Can I exchange an item?

expand_more

We currently do not offer direct exchanges. If you need a different size or color, please return your item for a refund and place a new order.
